# Design gap audit — dreamchat-frontend vs. the founder's visual reference
**2026-08-09 · READ-ONLY. No source file was changed and no PR was opened.**

Written after the founder loaded `:5173` for the first time and rejected the look.
This is a decision document: what the gap actually is, what each piece costs, and what must **not**
change because the law forbids it.

**Scope note (D-6).** This file is frontend-owned working product, like the handovers under
`docs/superpowers/`. It is **not** canon and copies nothing: the mockups stay in
`../dreamchat-world-backend/docs/20_design_ux/mockups/` and are referenced by path, never vendored.

## 0. Three findings that reframe the whole gap

### 0.1 The founder's design already exists in this repo, switched off

`src/ds/skins/fantasy.css:2` defines `[data-skin="fantasy"]`: **23 token overrides** — navy `#0d1320`,
gold `#c9a227`, **Cinzel** display, **EB Garamond** body, `--dc-glass-blur: 10px`,
`--dc-glow: 0 0 22px rgba(201,162,39,0.32)`, a two-layer `--dc-atmosphere`, a four-stop
`--dc-scene-scrim`, and `--dc-panel-sheen`.

**Nothing in the shipped app ever sets `data-skin`.** `setSkin()` (`src/ds/skins/index.ts:10-14`) has
exactly one caller: the dev-only `<select>` in `src/ds/gallery/Gallery.tsx:36`. `index.html:3` sets no
attribute; `src/router.tsx:28-45` applies only `applyTheme` (mood/ornament/accent). So every real page
renders the **base** skin — `--dc-bg: #f6f6f8` light grey, `#ffffff` panels, Georgia + `system-ui`
(`src/ds/skins/base.css:4-24`) — and six of the eight decorative tokens evaluate to `none`/`0px`.

The five Cinzel/EB-Garamond `.woff2` files are downloaded on **every page load**
(`src/ds/skins/fonts/fonts.css:1-10`, imported at `src/ds/styles.css:4`) and never used.

Proof, captured by setting the attribute at runtime in the browser and changing no file:

| | |
|---|---|
| `live/02-play.png` | as shipped |
| **`live/13-play-SKIN-fantasy.png`** | **identical build, `data-skin="fantasy"`** |
| `live/01-picker.png` → `live/14-picker-SKIN-fantasy.png` | white page → navy |
| `live/05-actor-dossier-viewer.png` → `live/15-dossier-SKIN-fantasy.png` | dossier |

Computed after the flip: `--dc-bg: #0d1320`, `--dc-font-display: "Cinzel", Georgia, serif`,
`--dc-glass-blur: 10px`, and the scene title's resolved `font-family` is `Cinzel`.

**One attribute closes roughly a third of the perceived gap.** It is the cheapest item in this
document by two orders of magnitude.

### 0.2 The first screen the founder saw is an unthemed light-mode page

The world picker is the only surface with no world, so `useWorldTheme(null)` → `clearTheme()`
(`src/router.tsx:30-32`) → base tokens. `live/01-picker.png`: **white background, black serif heading,
and browser-default blue underlined links**. The links are bare `<a>` elements inside a `Text`
(`src/pages/WorldsPage.tsx:83-86`) with no DS token at all — the same untreated-`<a>` pattern appears
at `src/pages/IndexList.tsx:54-58` and `src/router.tsx:51-69`.

There is no "DreamChat" mark, no world art, no chrome. This is the product's front door.

### 0.3 The single largest *structural* delta is blocked on the backend, not on us

Both references are **art-first**: a full-bleed painted scene fills the viewport and every piece of UI
floats over it on translucent glass (`pixel-perfect-companion/src/components/Layout.tsx:11-27` — image
+ tint + bloom + vignette + film grain). Our play surface has **no image at all**; the scene is a text
heading plus prose.

The plumbing is already here and unused: `AppShell` takes a `backdrop` prop that paints
`.dc-shell__backdrop` and lays `--dc-scene-scrim` over it (`src/ds/primitives/AppShell.tsx:44-55`,
`AppShell.css:10-12`), and `Page` forwards it (`src/pages/Page.tsx:34,45`). **No caller passes one, and
no caller can**: `scene_current/2`'s `place` object carries `{id, label, description, tone}` and **no
image field** — verified against `contracts/scene_current.v2.schema.json`. Participants have
`image_ref/1`; the place does not.

**This is a backend ask, and it should be raised today**: `place.image` as an `image_ref/1` on
`scene_current`. Everything else in this audit is ours.

## 3. TASTE vs LAW — what we may adopt and what we may not

**The references contain adjudicated violations.** The founder's own app implements several. Adopting
the *visual language* does not require adopting them, and this distinction must survive the restyle.

### 3.1 Present in the references — MUST NOT be built

| In the reference | Where | Rule | Why it stays out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Relationship to you"** card + trust slider | `mock_..._actor_seren_v2.png` | **B-3, B-4** | No relationship UI of any kind; the system never expresses the PC's feelings. |
| **Nav: "Relationships"** | `mock_gameplay_screen.png`; `companion/LeftNav.tsx:15` | **B-3** | Adjudicated. A router test asserts its absence. |
| **Nav: "Entities"** | gameplay mockup; `companion/LeftNav.tsx:13` | **GA-2, F-1** | DB vocabulary. Glossary says **Actors**. |
| **Nav: "Possessions"** | actor + location mockups | **GA-2, F-1** | Glossary says **Artifacts** / **Carrying**. |
| **`Current \| Previously \| Open Threads`** strip | gameplay mockup; `companion/RightPanel.tsx:32-34` | **GA-3** | A fixed taxonomy. The four aux-lens mockups are authoritative and show `Current / Inspect / Intent / Known`. |
| **High / Medium / Low severity** on threads | gameplay mockup; `companion` `--sev-high/medium/low` `styles.css:85-87` | **GA-3** + urgency ban | An urgency score the world does not compute. ⚠️ We already ship `--dc-status-high/med/low` (`base.css:19-21`) and a `Badge` bound to them — **defined, never used by a page**. Leave it that way. |
| **"Linked to"** panel | actor + location mockups | Locations AC#4 struck | — |
| **Location hierarchy tree** beside a breadcrumb | location mockup | **C-12** | One expression of hierarchy only. |
| **"Seen 1h ago"** | location mockup | **B-5** | Wall-clock never appears. In-world labels only. |

| **Interpretation confidence "High (82%)"** | `mock_aux_lens_intent.png` | D-7 | No confidence field exists in `beat_frame/2`. Inventing one fabricates a reading of the player's own words. |
| **Per-item "You could…" verbs** | `mock_aux_lens_inspect_artifact.png` | **D-14** | Must be backend-generated data tagged by kind. Same reason the Carrying overlay ships no verbs. |

### 3.2 Present in the references — pure taste, adopt freely

Cinzel display · Garamond-class body · a dedicated UI face · gold-on-near-black · gold hairline borders
at 32–65% alpha · gradient panel fills · the warm 1px inset top highlight · 18–28px radii · 4-layer
shadows with a gold bloom · film grain · floating islands with 20px gutters · gradient dividers that
fade at both ends · large circular portraits with rings and an active aura · slow ambient motion
(4.5s / 3.2s) · glass blur with saturation · full-bleed scene art under a scrim.

**None of these carries any semantic claim.** All are safe.

### 3.3 The one that needs a ruling

**A speaker-attributed "stage direction" line.** The reference's dialogue card renders a second,
italic, muted line beneath the speech (`DialogueCard.tsx:52-55`). Our `MessageSegment` has exactly one
text slot per message. `beat_frame/2` narration carries `{speaker_id, speaker_label, kind, text}` — one
text. Producing a second line client-side would be **inventing narration (D-7)**. If the founder wants
it, it is a backend field, not a restyle.

## 5. Proposed restyle plan

Ordered by *founder-visible change per unit of risk*. **Each pass ends in screenshots of every affected
surface for approval before the next begins.** Nothing starts without a decision on Pass 0.

### Pass 0 — decisions needed before any code (founder, ~15 minutes)

1. **Is `fantasy` the house skin, or the starting point for a new one?** It is 80% of the reference
   palette but its gold is duller (`#c9a227` vs `#febd56`), its radii are half, and it has no inset
   highlight or grain. Recommendation: **adopt it as the default now**, tune it in Pass 2.
2. **One skin or many?** Today skin and mood are orthogonal axes, and `mist`/`daylight` are *light*
