## Approach

**Token-first, port-back-friendly.** Your repo is plain CSS with `--dc-*` custom properties and a build test that fails on raw colours outside the skin files. So I will define every value as a `--dc-*` token in `src/styles.css` and map it into Tailwind's `@theme`, rather than scattering literals in components. The token block is then copy-pasteable into `src/ds/skins/fantasy.css` with no translation — the "single most valuable thing you can send" from `port-back.md`.

**Data-driven components with typed props.** Types are generated by hand from the vendored schemas (`world_directory/1` first), demo data is the fixture JSON verbatim. Every rendered string traces to a payload field. Nothing computed, nothing invented.

**The law is enforced structurally, not by memory.** No relationship UI, no severity taxonomy, no corrections UI, no tick, no wall-clock, no viewer selector, no create-world affordance, nav vocabulary fixed to Actors / Locations / Artifacts / Timeline. Missing fields surface as a visible `NEEDS BACKEND FIELD: x` note in the design, never as filler.

Fonts self-hosted-compatible, loaded via `<link>` in `__root.tsx`.
3. **Atmosphere primitives**: two-layer background gradient, scene scrim, 4.5% grain overlay, vignette, glass panel surface with inset highlight — all `prefers-reduced-motion` aware.
4. **World theme derivation**: `theme.accent` → strong variant, focus ring, and black-or-white on-accent by luminance; `theme.mood` and `theme.ornament` → atmosphere and motif with **silent degrade** for unknown words, never printed as text.
5. **Portrait slot primitive**: fixed-size, silhouette on `null`, layout does not shift when art arrives, URL built only from `path` + `?tier=`, byte-identical across renders (no cache-buster, no re-request on text change). Sized for real painted portraits, not the outdated fixture mosaics.

## Phase 2 — Surface 1: World picker

Route `/`. Payload: `world_directory.json` verbatim, both worlds.

Proposed direction — **a lit threshold, not a list**:

- A full-height midnight ground with the house atmosphere and grain; no world theme, because this surface has no world. This is the one place the base skin is seen pure.
- **Product identity**: a Cinzel wordmark with a hairline gold rule and a single quiet line beneath it, centred in a max-width column so two cards no longer cling to the left edge of a wide screen. The grid is centred and capped, so two cards read as deliberate rather than as five empty tracks.
- **World cards as lit glass plates**. Each card carries its world's accent as an edge light and a low wash behind the glass, not a 0.7em dot. The accent is derived, never hand-picked per world.
- **Mood becomes atmosphere, not a word.** `mist` and `nocturne` change the card's wash, grain weight and glow falloff. Unknown moods fall back to the house look silently. The literal words `nocturne` and `filigree` never appear — that is the §3.1 rule-5 fix.
- **Ornament becomes a motif.** `filigree` / `rivet` / `vine` / `circuit` each draw a corner flourish in the accent at low opacity; `none` and unknown values draw nothing. This is the open design idea the spec invites.
- **Two clearly different acts.** Entering to play is the card's primary act — a solid accent-derived control. Browsing the compendium is a secondary, quieter control. They stop reading identically, and both stay reachable per world.
- `display_name` renders verbatim, wraps cleanly to ~60 characters, never title-cased. "Mara 0A Fixture" stays ugly on purpose.
- `playable: false` keeps the world listed with the existing "Nobody to be here yet" chip and a dimmed, non-entering card.
- A world with a broken or missing accent falls back to house tokens and still renders.
- **No create-world affordance of any kind** — no button, no `+` tile, no empty-state CTA.
- Focus rings visible on every control, body text ≥ 4.5:1 checked against its actual background, ambient motion off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

`id` is used only to build the link target and is never rendered.

### Expected `NEEDS BACKEND FIELD` flags from this surface

I will confirm against the payload while building; likely candidates to bring to you:

- `world.tagline` / short world-authored mood line — the card has room for one line of world voice and the payload carries none. The mood word cannot substitute (it is engine vocabulary).
- `world.image.path` — a card-sized key art or crest slot. Today the accent is the only signal of what a world *is*.

Neither will be faked; the card composes correctly without both.
